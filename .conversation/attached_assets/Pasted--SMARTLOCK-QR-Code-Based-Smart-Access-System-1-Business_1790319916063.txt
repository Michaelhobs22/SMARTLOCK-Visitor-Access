# SMARTLOCK – QR Code-Based Smart Access System

### 1. Business Concept

**SMARTLOCK** is a smart residential access system that uses **QR codes and a sensor-enabled door lock** to provide secure, convenient, and contactless entry for residents and their visitors.

Instead of using physical keys, visitors can receive a QR code through the SMARTLOCK system. Once the QR code is generated, the visitor simply needs to **press “Activate Sensor” on the smart lock and present the QR code to the scanner**. If the QR code is valid, the lock automatically grants access.

### 2. How It Works

**Step 1 – Request Access**
A customer or visitor requests permission to enter the residence.

**Step 2 – Choose Access Type**

* **Premium Pass:** The visitor receives their QR code immediately without advertisements.
* **Normal User:** The visitor watches a short advertisement before receiving their QR code.

**Step 3 – Generate QR Code**
After completing the required process, SMARTLOCK generates a unique QR code for the visitor.

**Step 4 – Activate Sensor**
The visitor presses the **“Activate Sensor”** button on the smart lock.

**Step 5 – Scan QR Code**
The visitor presents the generated QR code to the lock's scanner.

**Step 6 – Access Granted**
The system verifies the QR code. If it is valid and authorized, the door unlocks automatically.

### 3. Problem

Traditional residential access methods can be inconvenient and difficult to manage. Physical keys can be lost, copied, or forgotten, while residents may also have difficulty controlling and monitoring visitor access.

Visitors may also need to wait for the resident to physically open the door or provide a key.

### 4. Solution

SMARTLOCK provides a **digital and controlled alternative** to traditional keys. Residents can provide visitors with temporary QR-based access, while the system can verify whether the QR code is authorized before unlocking the door.

This makes visitor entry:

* **Faster**
* **More convenient**
* **Contactless**
* **Easier to manage**
* **More secure than sharing physical keys**

### 5. Target Market

SMARTLOCK can be marketed to:

* Residential homeowners
* Apartment and condominium owners
* Boarding houses and dormitories
* Airbnb and short-term rental properties
* Property managers
* Small residential communities
* Rental properties

### 6. Revenue Model

SMARTLOCK can generate revenue through two main access models:

**Premium Pass**

* Users pay for a Premium Pass.
* They receive QR codes without advertisements.
* Premium users receive a faster and more convenient access experience.

**Normal/Free Access**

* Users do not pay for access.
* They watch a short advertisement before receiving their QR code.
* SMARTLOCK generates revenue from advertisers.

This creates a **freemium business model**, where users can either access the service for free by viewing advertisements or pay for a more convenient ad-free experience.

### 7. Key Features

| Feature                    | Description                                                                   |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **QR Code Access**         | Generates unique QR codes for authorized visitors                             |
| **Activate Sensor**        | Allows the visitor to activate the lock scanner before presenting the QR code |
| **Automatic Verification** | Checks whether the QR code is valid and authorized                            |
| **Automatic Unlocking**    | Unlocks the door after successful verification                                |
| **Premium Pass**           | Provides ad-free QR code generation                                           |
| **Advertisement Access**   | Allows normal users to generate a QR code after viewing an advertisement      |
| **Temporary Access**       | QR codes can potentially be configured with expiration times                  |
| **Contactless Entry**      | Visitors do not need to physically exchange keys or touch the door handle     |

### 8. Customer Value Proposition

> **“SMARTLOCK: Scan. Verify. Enter.”**

SMARTLOCK provides residents with a convenient way to manage visitor access while giving visitors a simple entry process. **Generate your QR code, activate the sensor, show your QR code, and enter.**
